Research addendum [personal salutation omitted] · 7 October 2026

The useful outcome is three explicit tests for whether the paper estimates can reach the actual
weighted sums. The algebra is exact; the asymptotic gains still depend on the manuscripts and on
matching the native objects to their hypotheses.

1 Measure the missing endpoint component
For the dilation-graph route, average the first endpoint over each mark fiber and keep the residual.
The paper estimate controls the average only under its stated hypotheses; a separate norm budget
controls the residual. This turns endpoint independence into a measurable application gate.

2 Charge weights without discarding masks
For fixed affine Liouville correlations, exact summation by parts charges the full variation of the
weight. Expand a genuinely fixed wheel into progressions first to avoid paying for every wheel
transition. This provides a conditional route for structured weights, but unrestricted bounded masks
can destroy cancellation.

3 Group complete weights before taking variation
For the quasi-RH route, combine signed pieces and complete partitions before taking absolute
values. Restrict to the true support of the character and retain both interval boundaries. This can
remove artificial variation costs while preserving real arithmetic-mask jumps. The attached
technical note records the exact budget and remaining analytical assumptions.

What this unlocks next
A complete nonzero native identity can now be tested against explicit endpoint, variation, modulus
and aggregation budgets. A toy pass is not a native theorem application. A single-factor Liouville
sum is not automatically a two-point correlation.
Status: Exact identities and small synthetic checks completed. Manuscript proof audits and scale-uniform estimates
are not certified. Native I112 remains BLOCKED_UNRUN; no user-computer execution or native weighted fixture is
represented here.

Dilation and affine gates
Fiber projection and residual budget
Let P average over each nonempty physical mark fiber, with the paper-defined equal state masses.
Write f⊥ = f − Pf. The projection decomposition is exact. Conditional on the physical-graph
hypotheses and the transference estimate applying to Pf and g:
|(f, Ag)| ≤ C X L−E+2C3 + LCabs ‖f⊥‖ ‖g‖.
Here L = log x and the row and column bound gives ‖A‖ ≤ LCabs. If ‖f⊥‖ ≤ εX X1/2LC3 and ‖g‖ ≤
X1/2LC3, sufficient gates for O(XL−κ) are E ≥ κ + 2C3 and εX = O(L−κ−Cabs−2C3). These are sufficient,
not necessary.
The actual physical measure, symmetrized operator, damping, phases, shared labels and exclusions
must match. The ideal hypothesis is a supremum over phase-dependent operator norms. Neither a
scalar mean nor testing only one phase can certify it. Integer-position masks preserve mark
independence; mark-list masks generally do not.

Fixed affine forms and full variation
Set s(n) = λ(a₁n+b₁)λ(a₂n+b₂), with fixed positive integer slopes, nonnegative integer offsets and
a₁b₂ − a₂b₁ ≠ 0. For integer L ≤ U define SL(v) = Σn=Lvs(n) and V(w) = |w(U)| +
Σn=LU−1|w(n+1)−w(n)|. Then exactly:
Σ w(n)s(n) = w(U)SL(U)

+ Σn=LU−1(w(n)−w(n+1))SL(n).
Thus |Σ ws| ≤ V(w) max |SL|. Conditional on the affine manuscript, the prefix maximum is bounded
by min{U−L+1, C′tuple U/(log U)c} for sufficiently large U. On [1,U], V(w) = o((log U)c) suffices for
o(U); this does not imply cancellation relative to a short interval length.
For a fixed wheel W, substitute n = Wm+r and sum the progression-wise variation budgets. Each
progression has its own fixed-form constant and the m=0 term is handled separately. Growing W,
growing form coefficients or many pieces require additional uniformity and the full aggregate cost.

Grouped quasi RH gate
Conditional prefix input
Let f(n)=λ(n)χ₆(n), where χ₆(n)=1 when gcd(n,6)=1 and 0 otherwise, and F(t)=Σₙ≤ₜ f(n). The derived
conditional input is |F(t)|≤Cε tᵅ, α=7/8+ε, with fixed 0<ε<1/8. It uses the manuscript's asserted
zero-free half-plane and a separate zeta/Perron argument. It is not a quoted numerical theorem; Cε
is unbound. The manuscript's Hecke lemma must not be relabeled as a Dirichlet-character lemma.

Pool the actual signed weights
Zero-extend every native contribution and set H(d)=Σᵣ cᵣ 1[d∈Iᵣ]Gᵣ(d). Keep all original parents,
multiplicities, support, ownership and hit factors. A complete cover reconstructs its parent only
when all children are summed. Removing selected children leaves a genuine selector weight.
List eligible positions t₁<…<tₘ in a finite block containing the support; put hⱼ=H(tⱼ) and q₀=t₁−1. The
exact global Abel identity is
T = F(tₘ)hₘ − F(q₀)h₁ + Σⱼ<ₘ F(tⱼ)(hⱼ−hⱼ₊₁).
Define Eα(h)=q₀ᵅ|h₁|+tₘᵅ|hₘ|+Σⱼ<ₘ tⱼᵅ|hⱼ−hⱼ₊₁|. Then |T|≤Cε Eα(h), conditionally. Signed pieces are
combined inside each jump before taking absolute values. Both boundaries of every block must be
paid; retain the unit at 1. Compression removes only the irrelevant χ₆-zero positions.

The sufficient gate and its limits
For an exact target Tₓ+Eₓ, the conditions Σblocks Eα(Hₓ)=o(X/log²X) and |Eₓ|=o(X/log²X) imply the
desired small-o discrepancy, conditionally. Neither budget is proved for I112. A finite calculation
cannot certify the scale-uniform statement, and a favorable calculation with Cε=1 cannot replace an
explicit valid constant.
For a squarefree fixed P, pure roughness has an exact convolution identity: f(n) 1[gcd(n,P)=1]
equals the sum of χ₆(d) f(n/d) over d dividing both P and n. Pool J(m) = Σ χ₆(d) H(dm) over divisors d
of P. Alternatively, pay the truncated cost Wα(U,P) = Σ χ₆(d) d⁻ᵅ over d dividing P with d ≤ U.
Growing-wheel cost can consume the power saving. Shifted endpoint masks and source-dependent
restrictions remain in the full weight. No automatic transfer to I117's different divisor kernel follows.

Evidence and reproduction
What was checked exactly
Dilation and affine controls: a two-state matrix kills the constant fiber vector but leaves an
unprojected pairing of 1; a reciprocal-prime rank-one kernel has mean 0 and norm 6;
repeated-prime congruences distinguish compatible from incompatible offsets. The Abel identity
and bound pass on all 300 subintervals of the N=24 synthetic Liouville example.
A decisive mask control uses s(n)=λ(n)λ(n+2) and w(n)=(1+s(n))/2. Exactly Σws=Σw=(N+Σs)/2. At
N=24, the unweighted sum is −6, the selected count and weighted sum are 9, and V(w)=12.
Conditional on the paper estimate, the selected sum is asymptotic to N/2, so an extension to all
bounded binary masks is false.
Grouped quasi-RH controls: 1,196 exact Abel/compression checks and 720 exact rough-convolution
checks pass. At 40 eligible positions, grouping a complete partition gives variation 1 while the
separate variations sum to 37. A genuine hit mask retains variation 15. Omitting the left boundary
gives 0 instead of −1 for the one-point interval [5,5].
